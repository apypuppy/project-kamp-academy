## Head Halter Mode: Instant Control for Pullers

If your dog’s go-to walking style is “zoom mode,” this feature is for you. The head halter configuration provides gentle control without harsh corrections. You’ll guide your dog from their head — not their neck — making it easier to redirect attention and reduce pulling.

Here’s how to set it up:

1. 1.  Slip the first loop over your dog’s head.
1. 2.  Adjust the ring so it rests comfortably under their chin.
1. 3.  Place the second loop over the nose and tighten between the ears.
1. 4.  Clip the safety to your dog’s collar for backup.

It’s like power steering for your pup.
